5 quick ways to verify news yourself

1. Check the source

Is the website a known publisher? Look for an "About" page and real contact details.

2. Read beyond the headline

Clickbait headlines often say something the article itself doesn't support.

3. Look for other coverage

Real major news is reported by several independent outlets.

4. Check the date

Old stories are often re-shared as if they were new.

5. Reverse-search images

Photos are frequently taken out of context from unrelated events.
