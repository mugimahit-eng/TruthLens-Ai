About the project

Fighting misinformation with explainable AI

TruthLens AI is a mini project that helps people judge whether online news and product reviews can be trusted, and shows exactly why.

Problem statement

Fake news spreads quickly on social media and messaging apps, shaping opinions on health, politics and safety. At the same time, fake and paid product reviews mislead shoppers and hurt honest sellers. Most people have no quick way to check what they read.

Our goal is a simple, fast, private tool that anyone can use: paste the content, get a clear verdict, and understand the evidence behind it.

Objectives

  • Detect likely fake news from text, headlines and URLs
  • Detect suspicious or paid product reviews
  • Give a score, confidence and plain-language reasons
  • Highlight evidence directly in the text
  • Keep user data private and on-device
  • Deliver a responsive, engaging 3D interface

Future scope

  • Machine-learning model

    Train a BERT / LSTM classifier on LIAR, FakeNewsNet and labelled review datasets.

  • Live fact lookup

    Backend service to fetch article text from URLs and match claims against fact-check databases.

  • Multilingual support

    Detection for Tamil, Hindi and other Indian languages.

  • Browser extension

    Check news and reviews directly on any website or shopping app.

  • Image & deepfake detection

    Flag manipulated images and AI-generated media.
